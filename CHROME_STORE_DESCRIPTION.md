# Alba - Chrome Web Store Listing

## Short Description (132 characters max)
Privacy-first AI impact estimator with prompt optimizer and daily footprint tracker.

## Detailed Description

### Make Every AI Prompt Count — With Full Environmental Transparency

Alba brings unprecedented visibility to the hidden environmental cost of AI. Every ChatGPT query, Claude conversation, Gemini search, and Perplexity question consumes energy, emits carbon, and uses water. Alba makes these costs visible in real-time, right where you work, and helps you reduce your impact without sacrificing productivity.

### 🌍 Why Alba?

AI is transforming how we work, learn, and create — but each interaction has a real environmental footprint. Data centers powering ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity consume massive amounts of electricity and water for cooling. Most users have no idea how much energy their prompts use or how to reduce their impact.

Alba changes that. It's the first Chrome extension that gives you live, per-prompt estimates of:
- **Energy consumption** (watt-hours)
- **Carbon emissions** (grams CO₂)
- **Water usage** (milliliters)

Whether you're a sustainability-conscious professional, educator, student, or simply curious about your digital footprint — Alba empowers you to make informed choices every day.

### ✨ Key Features

#### 📊 Real-Time Impact Labels
As you type your prompt, Alba instantly displays estimated energy, carbon, and water costs based on:
- Model size and type (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, etc.)
- Your geographic region's grid carbon intensity
- Current infrastructure and cooling requirements

No waiting, no external dashboards — just immediate, actionable insights.

#### ✂️ Intelligent Prompt Optimizer
Alba helps you get better results with less environmental impact:
- **Local optimization**: Instant suggestions to trim filler words and redundant phrases
- **AI-powered optimization**: Optional advanced rewriting that preserves meaning while reducing tokens
- **Before-and-after preview**: See exactly how much energy, carbon, and water you'll save before accepting changes
- **One-click accept**: Optimize your prompt with a single click

#### 📈 Daily Footprint Dashboard
Track your AI usage with beautiful, intuitive visualizations:
- **"Today" vs "This Chat"** comparison
- Day-over-day progress tracking
- Floating widget for at-a-glance stats
- Browser popup for detailed breakdowns
- CSV export for personal records or reporting

#### 🎉 Spotify-Style "Wrapped" Recap
Celebrate your environmental wins with shareable recap cards showing:
- Total energy, carbon, and water saved
- Equivalent real-world comparisons (miles driven, trees planted, etc.)
- Personal milestones and achievements
- Generate on-demand whenever you want to see your impact

#### 🔒 Privacy-First Architecture
Your data stays yours:
- **No backend servers** — all calculations happen locally in your browser
- **No data collection** — we never see your prompts or usage patterns
- **Optional AI features** — advanced optimization is opt-in and uses GitHub Models API
- **Open source** — full transparency with publicly available code

#### ⚙️ Fully Configurable
Tailor Alba to your needs:
- Select your geographic region (Global, US, EU, APAC) for accurate carbon calculations
- Choose model profiles (small, balanced, large)
- Toggle features on/off per site
- Light and dark themes
- Export or reset your data anytime

### 🚀 How It Works

1. **Install Alba** from the Chrome Web Store
2. **Visit your favorite AI chat platform**: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity
3. **Start typing** — Alba automatically displays impact estimates as you compose your prompt
4. **Review optimizer suggestions** (if enabled) and accept optimizations with one click
5. **Track your progress** via the floating widget or browser popup
6. **Generate Wrapped recaps** whenever you want to celebrate your environmental wins

Alba works seamlessly across:
- ✅ ChatGPT (chat.openai.com, chatgpt.com)
- ✅ Claude (claude.ai)
- ✅ Gemini (gemini.google.com)
- ✅ Perplexity (perplexity.ai)

### 📚 Evidence-Based Methodology

Alba's calculations are grounded in peer-reviewed research and authoritative data sources:
- **Energy consumption**: Based on benchmarked watt-hours per 1,000 tokens across model sizes
- **Carbon emissions**: Regional grid carbon intensity from Ember Global Electricity Review, IEA, and Electricity Maps
- **Water usage**: Consumption factors from NREL, U.S. EIA, USGS, and international energy agencies
- **Model profiles**: Derived from recent academic papers on LLM energy consumption

All methodology and sources are documented in the project's GitHub repository for full transparency.
