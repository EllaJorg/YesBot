Stop Getting Told What You Want to Hear — Live Sycophancy Detection for AI

AI assistants are trained to please. That means they often agree with you, validate your ideas, and soften criticism — even when you're wrong. This extension watches every response from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity in real time and scores how sycophantic it is, so you can actually trust the answers you're getting.

HOW IT WORKS

A floating widget appears as you chat, displaying a live sycophancy score from 0 to 1 — where 0 is truthful and 1 is pure flattery. Click the widget for a full breakdown of exactly what triggered the score: unnecessary agreement, excessive hedging, reversed positions, unearned praise, and more.


KEY FEATURES

Live scoring — every AI response is rated the moment it arrives, no setup required
Works across platforms — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity supported out of the box
Lightweight & private — no backend, no data collection, all analysis runs locally

WHY IT MATTERS

Sycophantic AI is a subtle problem. It feels helpful in the moment, but it erodes your ability to use AI as a genuine thinking partner. Whether you're making decisions, getting feedback on your work, or just trying to learn something — you deserve honest answers.
Install it, and find out how often you've been flattered instead of helped.
